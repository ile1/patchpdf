## What you should do in forks

1. Use your own name and icons for any public or commercial deployment.
2. Do not copy official site branding into your fork.
3. You may state that your project is based on this open source engine and
   link to this repository.
4. Do not present a fork as martialgames.net or as Martial Systems LLC.
